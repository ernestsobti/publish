> [!danger] Anarchy vs Anarchism
> Anarchy means A state of society without government, authority, or laws.
Usually implies chaos, lawlessness, or breakdown of control.

## Definition
- Anarchism is a political theory that is skeptical of the justification of authority and power. 
- Anarchism is usually grounded in moral claims about the importance of individual liberty, often conceived as freedom from domination.

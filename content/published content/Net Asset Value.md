## Definition


"Net asset value," or "NAV," of an [investment company](https://www.investor.gov/additional-resources/general-resources/glossary/investment-company) is the company's total assets minus its total liabilities. For example, if an investment company has securities and other assets worth $100 million and has liabilities of $10 million, the investment company's NAV will be $90 million. Because an investment company's assets and liabilities change daily, NAV will also change daily. NAV might be $90 million one day, $100 million the next, and $80 million the day after.


---


$$
\text{NAV}=\text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities}
$$
